## Replacing artwork

Replacement images placed in `Documents\Yomitoki\custom-assets\` stay on your computer and are never committed.
If you contribute replacement artwork to this repository:

- You must hold the rights to publish and redistribute it under CC BY 4.0.
- If it was made with an AI image service, check that service's terms first. Some services do not allow public redistribution.
- Never contribute artwork that copies or traces an existing manga or character.
