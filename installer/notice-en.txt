Yomitoki
A personal, multilingual manga screenshot translation reader

Usage notice
- Yomitoki is for personal reading and comprehension only.
- You are responsible for following the terms of service of any site you read on, and the copyright law where you live.
- Do not distribute translated output. Keep it on your own computer.
- Yomitoki only processes screenshots you capture yourself. It never downloads, intercepts or reconstructs images from websites.

After installing
- The first-run wizard downloads the translation components and models (about 3-5 GB, internet required).
- Translation runs through your own signed-in Claude Code and uses your Claude subscription.
- Screenshots never leave your computer; only the recognized text is sent to Claude Code.
